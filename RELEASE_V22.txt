BAM Dealer Enterprise Cloud v22.0 - Dealer Intelligence Edition

New in v22:
- VIN decoding with automatic vehicle specifications (external decoder, graceful fallback)
- Dealer Intelligence page for each vehicle
- AI-generated advertisements when OPENAI_API_KEY is configured; local professional draft fallback
- Market pricing suggestions based on recorded BAM vehicle sales and vehicle cost data
- Parts pricing suggestions based on recorded BAM parts sales
- Duplicate VIN / registration / stock detection before saving vehicles
- Customer purchase history combining vehicle and part purchases
- Smart reminders for registration, service, aged stock and draft invoices
- Business KPI dashboard for customers, reminders, parts revenue and stock age
- Additive database migrations only; existing v21 data preserved

Optional Azure settings:
OPENAI_API_KEY=<your OpenAI API key>
OPENAI_MODEL=gpt-5
BAM_VIN_DECODER_URL=https://vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov/api/vehicles/DecodeVinValuesExtended/{vin}?format=json

Notes:
- Market and parts pricing are advisory only and never overwrite your asking/selling price automatically.
- VIN coverage depends on the external VIN provider and can be limited for some Australian/older vehicles.
