BAM Dealer Enterprise v24.4 — CRM + Communications

New in this release
- CRM Centre for customers, leads and VIP customers
- Customer files with purchase history, email history and activity timeline
- Follow-up dates and due follow-up dashboard
- Lead source, tags, company and preferred-contact fields
- Log phone calls, emails, enquiries, quotes, visits and notes
- Optional BAM reminder creation from CRM activities
- Convert a saved Email Centre message directly into a CRM follow-up
- Direct links between Email Centre and customer files
- Existing vehicle, parts, workshop, VIN intelligence and financial data preserved

Database upgrade
- Additive contact columns only
- New crm_activities table
- Additive Email Centre metadata columns
