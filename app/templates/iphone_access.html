Mobile Access

Use BAM on Your iPhone

Your iPhone and Windows computer must be connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

Open BAM in Safari

  1. Leave BAM running on this Windows computer.
  2. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Open Safari on the iPhone.
  4. Enter this address:
{{ mobile_url }}

Log in with your normal BAM username and password.

Important: Windows may ask whether to allow Python through the firewall. Select Private networks and choose Allow access.

Add BAM to the Home Screen

  1. Open BAM in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it BAM Dealer and tap Add.

You will then have a BAM icon on your iPhone.

Access away from Wi-Fi

This version is designed for the same private Wi-Fi network. Do not expose port 5000 directly to the internet. Secure remote access requires HTTPS and a protected hosting service.
