# BAM Dealer Enterprise v15.2

## Tooling & Equipment Visibility Upgrade

- Moves **Tooling & Equipment** directly below Dashboard in the main navigation.
- Adds a prominent Tooling & Equipment panel to the Dealer Command Centre.
- Adds one-click **Add Tool** and **Open Tooling** buttons.
- Shows total equipment, current value, checked-out items, attention items and missing photos.
- Shows upcoming service, calibration and Test & Tag dates.
- Keeps all v15.1 equipment records, check-in/out history, photos and documents.
