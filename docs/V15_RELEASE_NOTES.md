# BAM Dealer Enterprise v15.0 — Professional Edition Foundation

## New Professional Asset Workspace
- Rebuilt the saved asset page into a clean workspace for cars, caravans, boats, motorcycles and trailers.
- Added quick navigation for Overview, Photos, Documents, Expenses, Workshop, Parts, Reminders and Sale & Profit.
- Added a consolidated financial snapshot showing purchase, expenses, workshop, service, parts, total invested and profit/loss.

## Document Centre
- Upload purchase invoices, registration papers, PPSR checks, roadworthy certificates, service history, warranty, insurance and manuals.
- Open or delete documents directly from the asset workspace.
- Document activity is recorded in the audit log.

## Reliability Improvements
- Removed duplicated/corrupted markup from the previous asset detail template.
- The asset route now loads all workspace information in one place.
- Version updated centrally to 15.0.

## Upgrade Safety
Keep your previous BAM folder as a backup. The included database is retained and the update uses safe existing tables.
