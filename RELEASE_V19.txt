BAM Dealer Enterprise Cloud v19.0

PROFESSIONAL PARTS CENTRE

New in Version 19:
- Automatic PRT-00001 style part numbering when SKU is left blank
- Dedicated part workspace with donor vehicle traceability
- Multiple part photo uploads with featured image support
- Part edit screen with condition, OEM number, barcode, position and fitment
- Rack / shelf / bin storage tracking
- Reserve parts for a buyer with reserved-until date
- Advanced Parts Centre filters by status, category and donor vehicle
- Sales history, parts revenue and gross profit per part
- Direct links between dismantling workspace and individual parts
- Donor stock number can automatically populate VIN, make, model and year
- Additive database migration preserves existing Version 18 data

Azure data remains under BAM_DATA_ROOT (/home/bam-data in the configured production app).
